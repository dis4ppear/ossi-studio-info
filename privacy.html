Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026.

Ossi Studio is a personal application operated by the owner of the connected video channels. This policy describes the application's use of Google and YouTube data.

Data accessed

With the operator's Google authorization, Ossi Studio accesses the selected channels' identifiers, video titles, descriptions, publication status and other video metadata, together with available YouTube Analytics reports such as views, watch time, engagement, traffic sources and aggregated audience information. It also stores OAuth access and refresh tokens so authorized background tasks can continue without repeated sign-in.

How the data is used

The application uses this information to display and compare video performance and carry out the operator's requested video uploads, scheduling and metadata or visibility changes. It does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to develop or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Ossi Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and disclosure

OAuth tokens, collected reports, local logs and video files are stored in the operator's local application data on their computer. API requests and authorized uploads are sent to Google and YouTube. User-directed AI assistance may process selected information with the operator's configured AI provider to perform the requested application functions. The operator controls that provider and its account settings. Ossi Studio does not publish channel reports or OAuth credentials on this website.

Retention and control

Local reports and credentials remain until the operator deletes them from the application data or removes the application. The operator can revoke Google's authorization at any time through Google Account permissions. Revoking authorization prevents further authorized API requests; existing local reports can be deleted separately. Ossi Studio does not ask for or store a Google account password.
